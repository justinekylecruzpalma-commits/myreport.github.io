Introduction

Product services refer to the activities, solutions, and support provided by organizations to enhance the value, performance, and usability of products. These services extend beyond product development and delivery by ensuring proper operation, maintenance, and continuous improvement throughout the product lifecycle.

In engineering industries, product services play an important role in maintaining product reliability, improving customer satisfaction, and providing technical solutions. These include installation, maintenance, technical support, customization, and consultation.

For Computer Engineers, product services involve designing, developing, implementing, and maintaining technological solutions such as hardware systems, software applications, networks, embedded systems, and cybersecurity solutions.

Product Overview

Products can be classified into different categories based on their purpose, function, and application.

Physical Products

Tangible products designed and manufactured to meet user needs.

Examples: Computer hardware, electronic devices, communication equipment.

Digital Products

Technology-based solutions delivered through software and digital platforms.

Examples: Applications, software systems, cloud solutions.

Engineering Solutions

Innovative solutions developed to solve technical and operational problems.

Examples: Automation systems, smart devices, integrated solutions.

Computer Engineering Products

Computer Hardware Systems

Computer engineers develop computer systems, embedded devices, microprocessors, IoT devices, and electronic components.

Software Applications

Includes software systems, mobile applications, databases, and automation solutions.

Network Systems

Includes communication systems, network infrastructure, servers, and cloud-based technologies.
