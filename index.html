01 • INTRODUCTION

Understanding Product Services

Product services refer to the activities, solutions, and support provided by organizations to improve product performance, reliability, usability, and customer satisfaction.

These services extend beyond product development by ensuring proper installation, maintenance, technical assistance, and continuous improvement throughout the product lifecycle.

Role in Engineering

In engineering industries, product services play an important role in maintaining technical solutions and ensuring that products operate efficiently. These include system support, maintenance, customization, consultation, and improvement services.
