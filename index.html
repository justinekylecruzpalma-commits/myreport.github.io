Introduction

Product services refer to the activities, solutions, and support provided by organizations to improve product performance, usability, and customer satisfaction. These services ensure that products continue to provide value throughout their lifecycle.

In engineering industries, product services help maintain reliability, efficiency, and proper operation of technical solutions. These include installation, maintenance, technical support, customization, and consultation services.

For Computer Engineers, product services involve designing, developing, implementing, and maintaining technological solutions such as hardware systems, software applications, networks, embedded systems, and cybersecurity solutions.
